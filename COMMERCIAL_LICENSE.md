**BMI Stellar** is dual-licensed. The complete source is available to
everyone under the [AGPL-3.0-only](LICENSE): use it, study it, fork it,
run it, and return your improvements to the community — that path costs
nothing and never will. This document is the paid path for teams and
businesses whose use cannot carry the Affero bargain (releasing derived
works, including network services, under the same license), or who need
the certainty of a conventional signed commercial grant.

The ladder below is the **White-Label Focus** ladder: it is built
around one premium tier — the right to ship BMI Stellar's validated
local-first data chain inside your own product, under your own brand.

**Why BMI Stellar is priced like architecture, not an app.**
BMI Stellar is not a health calculator. It is a local-first ledger with
AES-256-GCM encryption, Argon2id key derivation, and signed exports —
the same compliance stack that enterprise health platforms charge
$60K–$400K/year to host. BMI Stellar delivers the security without the
server, which means the enterprise eliminates HIPAA/GDPR server
liability entirely. The white-label tier licenses the pattern: the
validated data chain, the encryption stack, the PWA offline shell, and
the gatekeeper that keeps it honest. That is not an app. That is
infrastructure.

### Why the entry tier is $1,899, not $99.

BMI Stellar is not a hobbyist app. It ships four frozen, public audit
records covering architecture, privacy, and infrastructure, a signed
license certificate for every grant, and a gatekeeper suite that keeps
every claim in this repository checkable. Its commercial buyer is a
professional whose local-first infrastructure value pays off the
Professional license in one shift, not a hobbyist whose commercial use
is small and personal — the Personal tier (Free, AGPL-3.0) already
covers hobby, learning, and open-source contributions completely. The
Professional tier ($1,899/year) is the entry point for commercial use,
carrying the same target the $99 Individual tier served (solo devs,
freelancers, revenue under $100K/year).

The anchor matches the owner's fleet standard: zelynic and zejtron both
open their commercial ladders at Professional $1,899/year. The $99
Individual tier is retired as of this revision.

The standing grandfather pattern covers the retirement: any
Individual-tier customer who bought before this revision is
grandfathered at $99 for the term they bought — no existing customer
pays more than they agreed to.

## What Each Tier Grants

Every paid tier grants the same underlying asset — the full BMI Stellar
source, the validated data chain, the crypto stack, the PWA shell, and
the gatekeeper — with these differences in legal scope:

| Tier                | Closed-source use | Redistribution                       | Seats     | Support          |
| ------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------- | ---------------- |
| Personal            | No (AGPL applies) | AGPL terms only                      | Unlimited | Community        |
| Professional        | Yes               | None                                 | Up to 5   | Priority         |
| Business            | Yes               | None beyond your own workforce       | Up to 25  | Priority         |
| Enterprise Internal | Yes               | **None** — internal use only         | Unlimited | Priority + inbox |
| White-Label / OEM   | Yes               | Embed and resell in shipped products | Per grant | Priority + inbox |

- **Closed-source use** means your modifications and deployments are not
  required to carry the AGPL license while the grant is active —
  including operating BMI Stellar as a network service, which is the
  obligation AGPL §13 would otherwise impose.
- **Redistribution** means shipping the code (modified or unmodified) to
  third parties as part of a product. Enterprise Internal explicitly
  excludes it. The White-Label / OEM tier is the only tier that grants
  it, and it applies to the product named in your license certificate.
- **White-Label / OEM** ships under **your own brand**. The BMI Stellar
  name, logo, and Marks stay reserved per [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md);
  using them in your product requires a separate written approval.
- Seat counting covers the humans writing or deploying the software, not
  its end users. End users of your deployed instance need no license.

## Eligibility and Tier Boundaries

- Revenue is measured as the licensee's gross revenue (or total funding,
  for pre-revenue companies) in the most recent completed calendar year.
- Crossing a tier boundary takes effect at the next renewal — no
  mid-term clawback, no surprise invoices. Staying within your declared
  tier while exceeding its boundary breaches the grant.
- One licensee per grant. Reselling the license itself is not permitted
  in any tier; only the White-Label / OEM tier permits reselling the
  software embedded in a product.

## Terms, Renewal, and the One-Time Option

- Annual tiers run for 12 months from the license certificate date and
  cover every release published during the active term.
- The **White-Label / OEM one-time option** is a perpetual grant to the
  release current at purchase, plus optional maintenance at 20% of the
  one-time fee per year for continued updates and support. Without
  maintenance the grant remains valid; it simply stops receiving new
  releases.
- Every paid tier is delivered with a signed license certificate naming
  the licensee, tier, term, and grant scope. That certificate is your
  proof of licensing.
